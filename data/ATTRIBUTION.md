# Data attribution

Road and boundary data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the [Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). See <https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright>.

This covers:

- `fixtures/colombo.osm.pbf`, a small extract of central Colombo main roads and the Western Province boundary, used in tests.
- The `road_segment` table and anything published from it (road shapes, segment lengths, province assignment). If that database is published, the ODbL share-alike terms apply to it.

Votes collected by RouteRank are RouteRank's own data and are not covered by the ODbL.

Extracts are downloaded from [Geofabrik](https://download.geofabrik.de/asia/sri-lanka.html).
